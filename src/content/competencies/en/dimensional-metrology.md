## Precision for Your Product – Contract Measurement Services

Using the **FARO Laser Tracker** and **FaroArm**, we measure components, machinery, and industrial systems with precision and flexibility—at our facility or directly at your site.

The FaroArm is a portable coordinate measuring machine (CMM) designed for flexible 3D inspections. The FARO Laser Tracker captures three-dimensional points using laser distance measurement, achieving an **absolute 3D point accuracy of up to 0.03 mm**.

Laser tracker measurement combines **mobility, flexibility, cost-effectiveness, accuracy, and reliability**.
