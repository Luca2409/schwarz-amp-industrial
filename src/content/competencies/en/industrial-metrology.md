## Precision for Your Product – Contract Measurement Services

Using a **FARO Laser Tracker** and **FaroArm**, we measure components, machines and systems precisely and flexibly – either at our facility or directly at your site.

The FaroArm is a portable coordinate measuring machine (CMM) for flexible 3D inspections. The FARO Laser Tracker captures three-dimensional points by means of laser distance measurement with an **absolute 3D point accuracy of up to 0.03 mm**.

Laser tracker measurement combines the benefits of **mobility, flexibility, efficiency, accuracy and reliability**.
