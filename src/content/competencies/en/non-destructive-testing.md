## Testing Services Tailored to Your Needs

Our methods and procedures reflect the latest state of the art. Our services range from incoming and outgoing inspections and dimensional checks to production-integrated quality assurance and specialized testing procedures.

We perform conventional non-destructive testing in accordance with current technical standards, either at our testing center or directly at your site.

### Testing Methods

- **Radiographic Testing [RT]**

  Our mobile X-ray systems and isotope equipment are ready for a wide range of applications. A large radiographic testing bunker is also available at our facility.

- **Ultrasonic Testing [UT]**

  Using advanced equipment, we can inspect your components directly on site.

- **Penetrant and Magnetic Particle Testing [PT / MT]**

  We use electromagnetic testing equipment with direct and alternating current, including a test bench and yoke magnet. A custom-designed system is available for penetrant testing of high-volume parts.

- **Visual Testing [VT]**

  Data from the endoscope can be saved directly to a laptop.

### Additional Testing Methods

- **Leak Testing [LT]** – We perform helium leak testing both at our facility and on site using dedicated testing equipment.
- **Positive Material Identification [PMI]** – We identify and analyze materials using advanced technology.
- **Hardness Testing [HT]** – Hardness measurement according to the Vickers and Brinell methods.
- **Ferrite Content Measurement [FT]** – Ferrite measurement on materials and welds using a Ferritscope.
- **Surface Roughness Measurement [Ra]** – Measuring surface roughness on materials and welds.
- **Wall Thickness Measurement** – Determining the wall thickness of components.
- **Coating Thickness Measurement** – Ultrasonic measurement of coating thickness.

### Codes and Standards

Testing in accordance with **DIN, EN, ISO, and ASME**, including the
**ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section V**.

### Inspections

- Construction and weld inspection
- Industrial plant inspections
- Inspection of piping systems
