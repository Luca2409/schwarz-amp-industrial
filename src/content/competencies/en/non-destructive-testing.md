## Services Tailored to Your Needs

Our methods and procedures reflect the current state of the art. Our range of services extends from incoming and outgoing inspections and dimensional checks to in-process quality assurance and specialised testing procedures.

We perform conventional non-destructive testing in our testing centre or directly at your site in accordance with current technical standards.

### Testing Systems

- **Radiographic testing [RT]**  
  Our mobile X-ray systems and isotopes are equipped for every application. A large X-ray bunker is also available.

- **Ultrasonic testing [UT]**  
  Using state-of-the-art technology, we can also inspect your components on site.

- **Penetrant and magnetic particle testing [PT / MT]**  
  We use electromagnetic testing equipment with direct and alternating current, including a test bench and yoke magnet. A custom-designed system is available for PT testing of high-volume components.

- **Visual testing [VT]**  
  Data from the endoscope can be saved directly to a laptop.

### Additional Testing Methods

- **Leak testing [LT]** – We perform leak tests using helium testing equipment, both at our facility and at your site.
- **Positive material identification [PMI]** – We identify and analyse materials using modern technology.
- **Hardness testing [HT]** – Hardness testing according to Vickers and Brinell.
- **Ferrite measurement [FT]** – Ferrite measurement on materials and weld seams using a Ferritscope.
- **Surface roughness measurement [Ra]** – Surface roughness measurement on materials and weld seams.
- **Wall thickness measurement** – Determination of wall thicknesses.
- **Coating thickness measurement** – Ultrasonic coating thickness measurement.

### Codes and Standards

Testing in accordance with **DIN, EN, ISO and ASME**, including the
**ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section V**.

### Inspections

- Construction and weld monitoring
- Plant inspections
- Inspection of piping systems
